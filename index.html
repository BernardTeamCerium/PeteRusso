About Pete

A builder’s mind. A leader’s discipline.

Pete Russo is a dynamic entrepreneur, visionary leader, and business executive whose career is a masterclass in starting small and scaling relentlessly. From humble beginnings running a vending machine business in New York City, his journey grew into a diversified portfolio of food service ventures and a multi-state commercial and residential real estate empire.

Today, Pete leads a top-tier financial planning and technology firm with hundreds of employees and over $450 million in annual sales. He attributes his success to two things above all: disciplined time management and the power of a truly synchronized team.

A certified Coach Practitioner in Neuro-Linguistic Programming and extensively trained in Emotional Intelligence, Pete now helps executive leadership teams reach their full potential — sharpening the core skills of leadership engagement and effective risk management.
